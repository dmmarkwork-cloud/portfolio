Hangar time

Internships

Dornier Technology Inc.
Aircraft maintenance engineer intern · 2023

Ten-plus base and line checks under PCAR Part 6 across A320, B737, ATR 72 and BAe 146. Access panels off and back on, fan cowls opened, fasteners removed and refitted, structural damage and corrosion inspected, dye penetrant and borescope runs, findings written up for the responsible engineer.

Also where I learned to safety-wire properly — the small, unglamorous detail that tells you whether a job was done by someone paying attention.

Alpha Aviation Group Philippines
Flight operations exposure · AAG

Time in the A320 full-flight simulator and on a light single's flight deck — seeing the aircraft from the crew's side of the bulkhead. Checklists, panel flows, and how a flight crew actually reads the systems an engineer signs off.

It changed how I write findings: the person at the other end is flying the thing.

How I got here

I like the part where the drawing has to be right.

I studied aeronautical engineering at Holy Angel University and licensed as a PRC Professional Aeronautical Engineer in 2024. Somewhere between a hangar internship and a mesh study I worked out what I actually enjoy: taking an analysis far enough that it turns into a toleranced drawing someone can quote, machine and inspect.

The hangar came first, though — and it is hard to over-report a factor of safety after you have seen what an aircraft actually looks like at 40,000 cycles.

I am based in Tsukuba, Japan, and happy to walk through any of these end to end — including the two that failed.
