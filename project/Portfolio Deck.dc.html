01 · What changed in v2.0

A converged wrong quantity is still the wrong quantity.

v1.0 reported a factor of safety of 42.70 at the throat. That figure was wrong by roughly 6× for two independent reasons — and its mesh convergence study was perfectly sound, which is exactly the trap.
ERROR 1 — LOCATION
The throat was assessed. The chamber-to-converging junction governs, at 1.72× the throat stress.
ERROR 2 — QUANTITY
A body-scoped probe maximum is not a classified stress. Replaced with linearised membrane + bending on a through-thickness path.
DISPOSITION
v1.0 is preserved under its tag, not overwritten. Fourteen changes are itemised in the README.

02Pressure vessel · FEA · convergence

Thin-walled Al 6061-T6 propellant tank

3 mm wall, R = 147 mm, hemispherical caps, 3.25 MPa internal. CalculiX with C3D10 tetrahedra, hand-calc baseline, three-level mesh study, documented BC rationale.
Reported as a FAIL. FoS 1.91 against a self-set 2.00 at the cylinder-to-cap junction; 3 mm → 3.2 mm closes it.
An earlier model fixed the junction edge — the highest stress zone — and read 204.1 MPa. Constraining two hemisphere tip vertices instead removed the artificial stiffness: 144.5 MPa.

How I work

A result is only as good as the gate it passed.

01 — GATED PHASES
Hand calc before CAD, CAD before mesh, mesh before load. Each phase validates against the one before it.
02 — CLASSIFIED STRESS
Linearised membrane + bending on a path, not a probe maximum. Singular peaks identified and excluded.
03 — CONVERGENCE OR A BAND
0.62 % where refinement converges; a 7.02–7.12 band where it does not, rather than a false single figure.
04 — FAILURES STAY VISIBLE
Two of seventeen gates failed; the tank misses its own target at 1.91. Published, with consequence bounded.
05 — TRACEABLE VALUES
Every dimension, tolerance and fit tied to a source and a claim tier in a value register.
06 — MAKEABLE OR IT ISN'T DONE
GD&T sheets, stack-ups closing on frozen geometry, machining route, inspection plan, BOM, RFQ.
