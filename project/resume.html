Projects

Uncooled SS316 Convergent-Divergent Nozzle

  • Carried an uncooled SS316 C-D nozzle (Pc = 2 MPa, Tc = 800 K) from CFD through one-way coupled thermal-structural FEA into a GD&T drawing set; linearised membrane-plus-bending stress placed the governing location at the chamber-to-converging junction (29.6 MPa), not the throat, giving yield FoS 7.12 across a three-level mesh convergence study.
  • Passed CFD checks at peak wall y+ 1.74, discharge coefficient 0.977 and exit Mach 2.85 against 2.94 by hand calculation; identified creep at a ~527 °C wall, not static yield, as the real design driver.
  • Sized an 8× M8 A286 bolted joint per ASME BPVC VIII-1 Appendix 2 and detailed the package in CATIA V6 (CDN-000 to CDN-002) with tolerance stacks closing at 1.68×, machining route, inspection plan and BOM; a self-review against ASME Y14.5-2018 logged 5 blocking findings, revised after external peer review.
  • Published the v1.0 error (FoS 42.70, traced to a wrong assessment location and stress misclassification) alongside the corrected v2.0.

Al 6061-T6 Propellant Tank: Static FEA Verification

  • Ran static linear FEA of a 3 mm-wall tank with hemispherical heads at 3.25 MPa (inner radius 147 mm, R/t = 49) in CalculiX via PrePoMax (C3D10); mid-cylinder von Mises 136.6 MPa against 137.91 MPa by thin-wall hand calculation (1.0%), with a 0.62% mesh change at 264,007 nodes.
  • Reported FoS 1.91 against a self-defined 2.0 target at the cylinder-to-head junction, a 3D discontinuity effect thin-wall theory does not capture, and recommended a 3.2 mm wall; documented a boundary-condition correction that moved constraints off the junction (peak 204.1 to 144.5 MPa).

MK-10 Light Aircraft: FAR Part 23 Design and Structural Sizing

  • Held one design baseline from mission definition through weight and CG, drag buildup and the V-n envelope for a two-seat light aircraft (1,649 lb gross weight, 126.5 ft² wing, aspect ratio 6.58, NACA 2415, 125 BHP); verified 459 nmi range, 7.63 h endurance and 9,536 ft service ceiling.
  • Sized front and rear wing spars by hand calculation per Bruhn from a 10-strip spanwise load distribution through shear and moment diagrams, then resolved control surfaces, landing-gear load cases and a SolidWorks three-view.

PANO-VISTA: Fixed-Wing UAV Design and Systems Integration

  • Five-person team project: modelled a tractor-configuration fixed-wing UAV in one SolidWorks master assembly and built it with brushless propulsion (A2212/6T 2200KV, 40A ESC, LiPo), an eight-channel receiver and four servos across three control axes; delivered exploded and orthographic drawings with BOM.

Two-Blade Wooden Rotor: Drawing Package

  • Drafted a Ø 71.10 in two-blade rotor by hand in orthographic projection, then rebuilt it parametrically in SolidWorks as a blade-and-hub assembly with dimensioned three-view; twist falls from 78° inboard to 24° at the tip; solidity 0.095.

High-Lift Device Demonstrator: Servo-Actuated Wing Section

  • Built a sectioned wing (cardboard ribs, aluminium-foil skin) with servos between the ribs driving ailerons and leading-edge slats from an Arduino Uno sketch in C++, operated from a base-mounted control interface.
